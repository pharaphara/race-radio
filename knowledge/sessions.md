# Judging a session

- A review opens on a one-sentence verdict against the plan, then short headed sections that have
  something to say (execution, intensity and load, best effort, technique, the morning before),
  then what comes next. [convention]
- Mention a figure only if it serves the verdict, a section or the next step. [convention, from
  Enduragent]
- Summary fields and lap times alone cannot establish how a session went or justify changing the
  next one. Without usable data, say so, ask how it felt and how hard it was, and change nothing.
  [convention, from Enduragent]
- A deviation from the plan is a fact, not a fault. A rep held longer on purpose is "longer", a rep
  abandoned halfway is "shorter". Within three percent of the target is on target: power meters
  are accurate to one or two percent. [convention]
- Session RPE is a valid measure of internal load. The athlete enters it on the watch; read it when
  it is there, never ask for it. [evidence: Haddad 2017, Foster 1998]
