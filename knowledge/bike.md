# Cycling

- W' is the finite work capacity above critical power. Its balance drains joule for joule above
  critical power and refills below it, faster the further below and the emptier it is; this is the
  differential form of Skiba's model. A balance that goes negative means the declared critical
  power or W' is too low. Figures from different critical power models differ and are never
  compared. [evidence: Skiba 2012, Skiba 2015]
- Variability (weighted average over average power) near 1.0 means a steady ride; high values mean
  surges, which cost more than their average suggests. [formula]
- Aerobic decoupling compares output per heartbeat between the two halves of a steady effort. Under
  about 5% on a long steady ride is the usual sign of a solid aerobic base. It is meaningless on an
  interval session. Heat raises it. [convention; durability: Maunder 2021]
- Indoor and outdoor power are not interchangeable (different devices, no cooling, no terrain);
  keep the source in every comparison. [convention]
- Left-right balance and pedalling efficiency are watched as trends at comparable intensity; a
  growing asymmetry is worth mentioning. [convention]
