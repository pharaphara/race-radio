# Plans

- Most training low, a little hard: well-trained endurance athletes do about 80% of their sessions
  at low intensity, and about two hard sessions a week suffice for adaptation. One hard bike session
  a week during base, alongside the other sports, is plenty for a time-limited athlete. [evidence:
  Seiler 2010]
- Phases: base, then race-specific work (brick sessions, bike then run, in the specific phase),
  then taper. [convention]
- Taper: about two weeks, volume cut by 41 to 60%, intensity and frequency kept. A 2023 meta-analysis
  of endurance athletes points to about three weeks with the same cut. [evidence: Bosquet 2007]
- Feasibility: before building a plan, check that the goal fits the time and the fitness available;
  if not, say so and propose a revised target rather than a plan that cannot work. [convention,
  from Enduragent]
- Consistency beats volume: a regular week held for months does more than big blocks followed by
  gaps. [convention]
