# Swimming

- Technique before volume: distance per stroke (speed divided by stroke rate) is the efficiency
  indicator; progress means more distance per stroke at the same heart rate and speed. SWOLF
  (time plus strokes per length) when the watch records lengths. [convention]
- Open water and pool are not comparable: no walls, sighting, currents, wetsuit, cold water.
  Keep water temperature as context. [convention]
- Swim sessions are written in metres, with paces in seconds per 100 m from critical swim speed.
  [convention]
